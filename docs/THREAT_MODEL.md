# Data and authority boundaries

## Present tooling

Maintained source, selected executable files and the locked tooling environment are inputs to repository controls. Received descriptors and inventories cannot select policy or acquire a lane. Checks inspect bounded local scopes and report fixed diagnostics. Byte identity differs from interpretation and physical qualification.

An inventory is not a concurrent filesystem snapshot or power-failure durability guarantee. Native children use finite argument vectors and direct-child deadlines; completed-output limits are not live volume ceilings. Foundation qualification is in progress.

## Future paired sources

Plans, containers, journals, state, energy and reports are supplied data. They do not grant execution, acquisition, signing, equipment control, provider access, spending or publication authority. Actual source, decoder, clock and instrument admission precedes use.

Preserve original lane identities, event indices, complete selected windows, both unmatched lists, actual state observability and measured-energy availability. No silent favourable subset, reused event, guessed clock, zero-filled state, invented energy or simulation relabelled as fabric is admissible.

Unknown law, numerical method, measurement chain or current trust cannot become positive eligibility. A signature does not establish fidelity. Shared evidence, equipment and operational recovery remain separate unimplemented boundaries; no isolation, scientific or durability guarantee is claimed here.
